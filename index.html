About Us

Delivering exceptional staffing solutions for mission-critical IT needs

Building Workforce

Hiring Associates is one of the fastest growing companies focused on providing staffing solutions to Telecom, Financial and Healthcare clients for their mission critical IT staffing needs.

We play a major role in identifying best fit human resources for our clients and act as a bridge between the client and the candidate. We help our clients successfully respond and capitalize on opportunities by providing professional services in the areas of systems integration and Application Development.

We believe that our clients expect value for money, and it is our goal to exceed their expectations, and provide cost effective recruitment and staffing solutions.

A Trusted Partner

At Hiring Associates, we don't fill requisitions - we provide recruitment strategies & human capital solutions. Creating an "ideal" candidate on paper is not the best way to start looking for a new hire.

People are the often overlooked factor in a company's business plan. We know that in order for your vision to be successful, you need a partner in human capital resources - not an order taker.

  • Achieve business goals with faster time to market
  • Flexible resource allocation for specialized projects
  • Faster project delivery with quality assurance
  • Cost-effective staff augmentation solutions
